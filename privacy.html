Privacy Policy for Site Ledger

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Site Ledger ("the app") is a record-keeping app for timesheets, pay, invoices and expenses, made by Evgeni Minchev, London, United Kingdom. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Short version

The app does not collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your own device. There is no account, no server, no advertising and no analytics.

What the app stores

When you use the app you may enter business details, client and site names, worker names and pay rates, hours, invoices, expenses and photos of receipts or completed work. All of this is saved only in the app's storage on your device. We do not receive it and cannot see it.

Camera and photos

The app can use your camera or let you choose photos so you can attach receipts and site photos. Photos taken with the Photo tool get the site name, date and time added to the picture. Photos are kept inside the app on your device, and a copy is saved in the Documents/SiteLedger/Photos folder on your device so you can find it in your Files app. They are not uploaded anywhere.

Backups and PDF files

When you tap Save backup, the app saves a file in the Documents/SiteLedger folder on your device. When you tap Send backup or Download PDF, the app creates a file and opens the Android share sheet. Where that file goes (for example email or cloud storage) is your choice and is handled by the app you pick, under that app's own policy.

Sharing, advertising and tracking

We do not sell, share or send your data to anyone. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. It does not need an internet connection to work.

Payments

If the app is paid, the purchase is handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete items inside the app, or remove everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover data you have deleted. Use Save backup to keep your own copy.

Children

The app is intended for adults working in construction and is not directed at children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. If you have a question about this policy, contact us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
